# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Curious learners, builders, and working technologists who want a clear route through difficult AI, machine learning, transformer, and video-generation concepts. They arrive from shared posts, search, or recommendations and need explanations they can understand, revisit, and apply.

## Product Purpose

AI Paglu is a public learning library built from practical study. It turns difficult ideas into useful articles, visual explanations, code notes, mockups, graphs, and revision material. Success means a reader can find a concept quickly, understand its mental model, and leave with something concrete to try or remember.

## Brand Personality

Curious, lucid, and opinionated. The voice is that of a thoughtful learner sharing a well-organized desk: serious about accuracy, comfortable showing work in progress, and never academic for its own sake. The visual identity may explore several selectable expressions while the reading experience remains steady.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI landing pages, neon-on-black cyberpunk styling, purple-gradient SaaS surfaces, excessive animation, inflated marketing claims, decorative dashboards, and layouts that make the author more prominent than the learning. Avoid a traditional magazine costume or a dense documentation portal that feels institutional.

## Design Principles

1. Make the concept the main character.
2. Pair every explanation with a useful artifact, example, diagram, or question.
3. Show a clear route from first principles to working intuition.
4. Make revision and rediscovery as easy as first-time reading.
5. Let the identity feel personal through judgment, structure, and detail rather than biography.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal compliance target is required. Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic structure, responsive layouts, reduced-motion behavior, and labels that do not depend on color alone as baseline product quality.
